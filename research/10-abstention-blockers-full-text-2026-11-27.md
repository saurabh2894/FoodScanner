## Addendum (cultures and brine), 2026-11-27 23:30 IST

Source: FSSAI full chapter PDFs fetched with a browser User-Agent (Ch 2.1 Dairy, Ch 2.3 Fruit and Vegetable products). New evidence for each revisit is stated; nothing here is a categorical scientific claim.

### Probiotic and combined cultures (N08)
- FSSAI 2.1.13 (fermented milks) item 2(b)(viii) lists "Prebiotics and Probiotics" as optional ingredients, apart from starter cultures (item 2(b)(i)). Item 6(c) says that when cultures of Bifidobacterium bifidum and Lactobacillus acidophilus and other lactic acid producing harmless bacteria are added, a declaration is made on the label. Ch 2.1 item 6 sends probiotic use to the FSSAI Health Supplements/Nutraceuticals Regulations 2016 (not fetched here).
- What this adds: identity of a probiotic culture as an optional fermented-milk ingredient is admissible for naming. What it does not add: a placement clause. The Group 1 clause the narrow milk-plus-culture rule leans on is plain yoghurt without additions (FAO 2019 Table 1 Group 1 "fresh or pasteurized plain yoghurt"); no fetched source says an added probiotic strain keeps the product within that clause or takes it out. Source silence, so held. tests/test_round_fixes.py::test_probiotic_culture_is_not_taken_for_a_starter_culture stays, and its reason (2.1.13 lists probiotics apart from starter cultures) is confirmed by the full text.
- N08 also fails a second, separate guard: the combined phrase "Active Lactic Culture and Probiotic Culture" is read as one unplaced item and the repo blocks unread culture text from producing a complete lower-group result (f01d110).
- Species names (Bifidobacterium lactis, Lactobacillus acidophilus): only B. bifidum and L. acidophilus appear in 2.1.13; B. lactis is not named in the fetched chapter. No rows added.

### Iodised salt brine (N11)
- FSSAI 2.3 defines "brine" in two places: packing medium "water in which common salt is added" (classification of packing media, the declaration "Brine" when salt is added to water) and 2.3 "packing brines" as "the solution of salts dissolved in potable water, with or without addition of some or all the ingredients specified in entry (i)" (open composition).
- What this adds: salt plus water is the base of brine in the canned fruit/vegetable standard. What blocks a row: the second definition leaves composition open, and "iodised salt brine" in a pickle is not one of the declared-packing-medium cases. Held, same rule as missing bracket detail.
- N11 would still abstain even with a brine row: it carries INS 330, INS 260 and INS 211 plus "Mixed spices powder" (FSSAI 2.9.20 allows added starch, salt, oils, sweeteners).
