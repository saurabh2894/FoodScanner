## Detail and open questions
1. Sprouted grains. FAO's process list does not include germination; "other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats or other food substances" is the only wording a sprouting step could fall under. This is an inference, not a source statement. Open: does the owner want the open clause accepted as a basis (PROVISIONAL, stated), or a naming source first?
2. Fruit and vegetable powders. The risky cases are blends and carrier-bearing powders ("dehydrated vegetable powder (garlic, onion, lemon)" with maltodextrin elsewhere in the list). A recognition row only says the NAME is a known food; the group decision stays with the label. Open: recognise base fruit/vegetable names (not powders) first, since powder forms already resolve through the descriptor words.
3. Vitamins and minerals. Group 1 wording reaches fortified flour, Group 2 reaches iodised salt; the NOVA Group 4 text in the same report lists additive classes but no vitamin compound. Open: whether recognition-only rows (kind neutral, no group effect) for named vitamin/mineral compounds are wanted at all, given the abstain policy.

## Verbatim findings
- Sprouting: the words sprout, sprouted and germination occur in NONE of FAO 2019, Monteiro 2018, Monteiro 2019 or the three NUPENS pages (0 mentions each; also 0 for "fortif" in FAO-2019-section-3 prose and in Monteiro 2019). The only wording that could cover a sprouting step is the open clause, FAO Table 1: "and other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats or other food substances to the original food." That is an inference for sprouting, not a source statement, so sprouted forms are NOT given a row.
- Fruit and vegetable powders: FAO p.6, Group 1 definition (corrected from p.10 by provenance_audit_1): "Minimally processed foods are natural foods altered by methods that include removal of inedible or unwanted parts, and also processes that include drying, crushing, grinding, powdering, fractioning, filtering, roasting, boiling, non-alcoholic fermentation, pasteurization, chilling, freezing, placing in containers, and vacuum packaging." Monteiro 2018: "fresh, squeezed, chilled, frozen or dried fruits and leafy and root vegetables ... milk, pasteurized or powdered". FDC (CC0) names one exactly: SR Legacy 173945 "Bananas, dehydrated, or banana powder". The definition names the PROCESS 'powdering' of natural foods, so a single powdered fruit or vegetable is covered as a form: rows added for banana powder, strawberry powder, beetroot powder (PROVISIONAL, exact terms not named by the clause). CORRECTED SCOPE (owner): the clause covers powders of single natural foods, but a bare label term does not establish a single-ingredient powder free of carriers or anti-caking agents, so the rows are kind `food_powder`, a conditioned kind: Group 1 ONLY as the sole item of a list, and every multi-item list that holds one abstains with the composition uncertainty stated. Absence of carriers is never assumed from the term.
- Vitamins and minerals: FAO Table 1 Group 1: "foods with vitamins and minerals added generally to replace nutrients lost during processing, such as wheat or corn flour fortified with iron and folic acid" (purpose-conditioned); Group 2: "group 2 items with added vitamins or minerals, such as iodised salt"; Group 4 (Monteiro 2018): "'health' and 'slimming' products such as powdered or 'fortified' meal and dish substitutes", and "notwithstanding the fortification of some ultra-processed products with some micronutrients". No source names the compounds. Fortification stays purpose-conditioned: no row, the list abstains.
- Also new for the next batch: Monteiro 2018: "Some of these are directly extracted from foods, such as casein, lactose, whey and gluten." (names whey and lactose as Group 4 ingredient sources).

## Open design decision for you (needs a code change, so not made here)
There is no kind that means "recognised by name, placement unsupported, the list must abstain". `neutral` (water) is ignored by the group logic, which would silently let fortification or a sprouted form drop out of the decision. A recognition-only kind (for example `unplaced`) would put "Vitamins and Minerals" and sprouted forms in the vocabulary while the group still abstains and the reason names them. It changes classify.py and the JS port; it is conservative (it only adds named abstentions) but is classification code, so I have not built it without your approval.
