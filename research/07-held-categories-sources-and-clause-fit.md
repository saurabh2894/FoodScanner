## Detail and open questions
1. Sprouted grains. FAO's process list does not include germination; "other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats or other food substances" is the only wording a sprouting step could fall under. This is an inference, not a source statement. Open: does the owner want the open clause accepted as a basis (PROVISIONAL, stated), or a naming source first?
2. Fruit and vegetable powders. The risky cases are blends and carrier-bearing powders ("dehydrated vegetable powder (garlic, onion, lemon)" with maltodextrin elsewhere in the list). A recognition row only says the NAME is a known food; the group decision stays with the label. Open: recognise base fruit/vegetable names (not powders) first, since powder forms already resolve through the descriptor words.
3. Vitamins and minerals. Group 1 wording reaches fortified flour, Group 2 reaches iodised salt; the NOVA Group 4 text in the same report lists additive classes but no vitamin compound. Open: whether recognition-only rows (kind neutral, no group effect) for named vitamin/mineral compounds are wanted at all, given the abstain policy.
