culinary-gaps-24 (general growth): potato starch, rice starch, wheat starch. Placement: FAO 2019 Table 1 Group 2 "starches extracted from corn and other plants", same basis and behaviour as the tapioca starch row. Identity (naming only): USDA NAL Agricultural Thesaurus concepts potato starch https://lod.nal.usda.gov/nalt/59403, rice starch /61521, wheat starch /64114 (type Product, broader: starch products). The original push caa3e17 also had himalayan salt, kosher salt, hemp seed oil and pumpkin seed oil. They were withdrawn: no admissible identity source found (CXS 150 food-grade salt names sea, rock salt and brine only; CXS 210 has no hemp or pumpkin seed oil; NALT has no hempseed oil, hemp oil, kosher salt or Himalayan salt, and its "pumpkin oil" is not the label "pumpkin seed oil"; FDC SR Legacy and CoFID have none). The two salts would also have been variants of existing salt rows. Modified and other starch forms stay held. Not staged: pink salt (curing-salt collision), cassava and sago starch, icing sugar, coconut and palm sugar, date syrup, treacle, golden syrup, ghee, tallow, suet, rapeseed oil.
