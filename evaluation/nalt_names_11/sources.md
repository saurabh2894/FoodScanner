# Source ledger, checked November22,2026

## NOVA placement, independent of identity

- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10127522/ , Martinez Steele et al., J Nutr2023, primary classification method/Table1. Plain vegetable/fruit class, nuts/peanuts/oilseeds without salt or sugar, named minimal grinding/roasting processes, and separate Group3 culinary additions. High confidence in the clauses, class-instance only for the new foods. No new strong marker. Current text read in slice9 and unchanged here.

## Whole pepper identity and form

- https://lod.nal.usda.gov/nalt/22077 , USDA NALT official current Product and explicit English names, CC BY4.0. High identity confidence, not NOVA. Both peppers/spices ancestry retained as a scope warning.
- https://www.extension.purdue.edu/foodlink/food.php?food=hot+pepper , Purdue Extension FoodLink, university food-use guidance. Explicit whole raw pepper, freezing/drying and grinding. Independent support for the physical vegetable form. Oil-containing roasting recipes do not support Group1.
- https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/CommoditiesCoveredbyPACA.pdf , USDA AMS fresh/frozen produce commodity list names sweet/chili peppers. Corroboration of food identity/form only. Its US legal scope is not generalized or used as a NOVA classification.

## Immature bean pods, not mature seeds or split dal

- https://lod.nal.usda.gov/nalt/16213 , USDA NALT official Product definition explicitly identifies immature succulent Phaseolus pods and names the reviewed snap/French/string/wax forms. High identity confidence, not NOVA.
- https://extension.purdue.edu/foodlink/food.php?food=snap+bean , Purdue Extension names snap/green/string beans and yellow wax types. High confidence for these whole-pod identities. Its salted cooking recipe is not Group1 evidence.
- https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/4255e/ , University of Maine Extension Bulletin4255, Vegetables and Fruits for Health: Green Beans and Wax Beans. Explicitly separates immature edible pods from mature dry beans. Food identity/form, not NOVA.
- https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-beans-home-garden , University of Maryland Extension, updated January20,2026. Young edible pod definition and growth habits across bean types. Supports holding bare bush/pole names rather than merging all such plants/seed forms into snap beans.

## Shea seed kernel, separate from extracted fat

- https://lod.nal.usda.gov/nalt/68207 , USDA NALT official current Product shea nuts, nuts ancestry, explicit sheanuts synonym. Identity only, not NOVA.
- https://www.fao.org/4/X6877E/X6877E09.htm , FAO Food Composition Table for Use in Africa, Nuts and Seeds, item466 dried shea seed kernel, separately press-cake467. Historical primary food-composition reference, high confidence for the named dried edible kernel, not a modern NOVA classification.
- https://www.fao.org/4/x6877e/x6877e23.htm , same table's edible-plant index binds shea-nut/lulu kernel/Butyrospermum parkii and distinguishes kernel, press-cake, pericarp and oil. Identity only.
- https://www.fao.org/4/y5918e/y5918e11.htm , FAO Unasylva article by Masters, Yidana and Lovett, shea products/trade. Explains butter extraction and fractions, used as counterevidence against treating extracted shea butter as whole-ground seed.
- https://apps.worldagroforestry.org/treedb/AFTPDFS/Vitellaria_paradoxa.PDF , World Agroforestry tree database, botanical/food-use corroboration, parboiled/dried seeds versus extracted fat. Not NOVA or evidence for every raw preparation.
