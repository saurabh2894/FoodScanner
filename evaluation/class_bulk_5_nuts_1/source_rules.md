# Category Source Rules: FDC 'Nut and Seed Products' (Batch 5)

## 1. Primary Source Authority & Verbatim Quotations

All classification grounding is established from the primary report:
**FAO 2019**: Carlos Augusto Monteiro, Geoffrey Cannon, Mark Lawrence, Maria Laura da Costa Louzada, Priscila Pereira Machado. *Ultra-processed foods, diet quality, and health using the NOVA classification system*. Rome: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, 2019. (Report text `ca5644en.pdf`).

### Verbatim Group 1 Clause
> **Table 1, Group 1: Unprocessed or minimally processed foods — Examples (p. 11)**  
> *"tree and ground nuts and other oily seeds (with no added salt or sugar)"*

### Verbatim Minimal Processing Methods Clause
> **Table 1, Group 1: Minimally processed foods — Definition (p. 11)**  
> *"Unprocessed foods altered by industrial processes such as removal of inedible or unwanted parts, drying, powdering, squeezing, crushing, grinding, fractioning, steaming, poaching, boiling, roasting, and pasteurization, chilling, freezing, placing in containers, vacuum packaging, non-alcoholic fermentation, and other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats or other food substances to the original food."*

### Conditioned Forms Are NOT Plain Forms
The salt/sugar restriction is an explicit, intrinsic part of the clause: **"(with no added salt or sugar)"**. Conditioned forms (salted, sugared, glazed, sweetened, or candied nuts/seeds) are explicitly placed into **Group 3: Processed Foods**:
> **Table 1, Group 3: Processed foods — Examples (p. 12)**  
> *"salted or sugared nuts and seeds;"*

Furthermore, oil-roasting introduces added culinary fat/oil, violating the Group 1 condition *"other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats to the original food"*. Extracted oils from seeds and nuts belong to **Group 2: Processed culinary ingredients**:
> **Table 1, Group 2: Processed culinary ingredients — Examples (p. 11)**  
> *"Vegetable oils crushed from seeds, nuts or fruit (notably olives);"*

### Inference Levels
- **Tree nuts** (`almonds`, `walnuts`, `cashews`, `pecans`, `hazelnuts`, `macadamia nuts`, `brazil nuts`, `chestnuts`, `pistachio nuts`, `pine nuts`, `acorns`, `beechnuts`, `butternuts`, `ginkgo nuts`, `hickory nuts`, `pili nuts`):  
  *Inference level: class-instance of tree nuts*. The FAO clause names the overarching class `"tree and ground nuts and other oily seeds (with no added salt or sugar)"`; individual species are class-instances.
- **Ground nuts** (`peanuts` / `groundnuts`):  
  *Inference level: class-instance of ground nuts*.
- **Oily seeds** (`sesame seeds`, `sunflower seeds`, `pumpkin seeds`, `flax seeds`, `chia seeds`, `watermelon seeds`, `lotus seeds`, `safflower seeds`, `breadfruit seeds`, `breadnut seeds`, `sisymbrium seeds`):  
  *Inference level: class-instance of oily seeds*.

### Silence on Derived Forms (`SOURCE-SILENT`)
Where the FAO 2019 report text does not name or define a specific derived form, the source is recorded as **SOURCE-SILENT**:
- **Nut pastes & butters** (e.g. peanut butter, almond butter, tahini): **SOURCE-SILENT**. (Queued pending per-form policy decision).
- **Nut flours & meals** (e.g. almond flour, chestnut flour, defatted sesame meal): **SOURCE-SILENT**. (Queued pending flour-kind extension decision).
- **Nut milks & creams** (e.g. almond milk, hazelnut milk): **SOURCE-SILENT** under Group 1; ultra-processed formulations under Group 4.
- **Sprouted seeds**: **SOURCE-SILENT** (neither permitted nor excluded in NOVA text, as established in Task 6).
- **Coconut**: **SOURCE-SILENT** in Group 1 Table 1 examples (standing hold in Foodscan).
