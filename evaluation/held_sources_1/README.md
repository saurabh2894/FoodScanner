# held_sources_1: conditioned powder rows (corrected scope)

The three rows (banana powder, strawberry powder, beetroot powder) are kind `food_powder`, NOT unconditioned whole_food (owner correction before merge). FAO p.6 (Group 1 definition; first cited as p.10, corrected by provenance_audit_1) covers 'powdering' of natural foods; a bare term does not establish that the powder is single-ingredient or free of carriers or anti-caking agents.
Mechanism (classify.py and the JS port, a small conservative code change): a `food_powder` is Group 1 only as the sole item of a list (nothing else listed, so no carrier is listed); in any multi-item list it makes the list abstain with the reason "a powder of a food (...) is named without its composition: the label does not say whether it is that food alone or carries carriers or anti-caking agents"; it never joins a Group 3 combination; existing strong markers keep priority (Group 4). Each row's source states the condition.
Gate (scripts/demand_batch.py, held-decisions-1): 66 frozen inputs unchanged on every compared field (0 unexplained); out-of-sample 32 unchanged vs main (no group, status, driver or reason change). Acceptance label: banana, strawberry and beetroot powder recognised, unassessed 6 -> 3, group still abstains ('only weak or unlisted markers present: antioxidant'); without the antioxidant item the powder abstention would apply.
Not rows: sprouted forms and vitamins and minerals (no source; recognition-only handling is a separate approved follow-up with its own constraints).
