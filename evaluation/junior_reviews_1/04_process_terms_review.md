## C. The substantive senior questions
**1. Sprouted: general Group 1 clause, or continued abstention?** Recommendation: the general clause places it, as an INFERENCE, per-term. Basis: FAO's Group 1 method list ends "and other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats or other food substances to the original food" (verified). Germination adds water and nothing named; a sole-ingredient sprouted grain is the original food after a method that adds none of the listed substances. It is not NAMED (0 hits in every NOVA source I hold), so the row must say 'exact term not named; inference from the general method clause'. Follow the owner's earlier discipline for dried/dehydrated forms: PER-TERM (sprouted ragi, sprouted jowar, sprouted moong...), never a free-floating 'sprouted' descriptor. Keep abstention for any 'sprouted X' with a bracket or co-ingredient adding something unrecognised (the bracket_residual and powder standards already do that). If you prefer the cautious reading, the current unplaced state stays; nothing here forces a change.
**2. 'Vitamins and Minerals' standalone declaration: neutral or abstain?** Recommendation: NEUTRAL (kind neutral: recognised, no group effect), recorded with two sources and the condition kept attached. FAO Group 1 definition: "foods with vitamins and minerals added generally to replace nutrients lost during processing, such as wheat or corn flour fortified with iron and folic acid" (verified); NUPENS Group 1: "the use of preservatives and fortifiers is accepted" (read in full). Condition: FAO says 'generally to replace nutrients lost'; a bare 'Vitamins and Minerals' item does not state the purpose, so the inference level is 'fortification by vitamins/minerals, purpose unstated'. Counter-evidence checked: PMC10127522 classes a corn-flakes example as ultra-processed on sugar/HFCS/malt flavour while 'vitamins and minerals' sit in the same list: the driver was the sugar, not the vitamins. So neutral-by-matrix is source-supported; it must never rescue a list that has another marker (markers keep priority).
**3. 'Fortified' on staple bases:** Recommendation: Group 1-compatible ONLY where FAO names the pattern: a flour/grain staple fortified with vitamins/minerals ("wheat or corn flour fortified with iron and folic acid", verified). Therefore a per-form row for 'fortified <staple flour/grain>' with the FAO clause, NOT a free descriptor: 'fortified' on a milk powder, a biscuit or a drink is not covered (the milk powder exception already abstains on 'fortified milk powder'). 'Fortified with' a non-vitamin/mineral (protein, fibre) is not covered. Scrutiny elsewhere: unrecognised additions on the same item abstain (bracket_residual). Current: 'Fortified Wheat Flour' is unrecognised (abstains); 'Wheat Flour (fortified with iron and folic acid)' answers Group 1 today (the bracket words are ignored by the plain-row path for the flour kind): worth a decision, because the same ignored-bracket gap I closed for legume/vegetable/fruit kinds exists for flours.
