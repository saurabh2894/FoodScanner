# Category Source Rules: FDC 'Fats and Oils' (Batch 7)

## 1. Primary Source Authority & Verbatim Quotations

All classification grounding is established strictly from the primary report in the repository:  
**FAO 2019**: Carlos Augusto Monteiro, Geoffrey Cannon, Mark Lawrence, Maria Laura da Costa Louzada, Priscila Pereira Machado. *Ultra-processed foods, diet quality, and health using the NOVA classification system*. Rome: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, 2019. (Report text `ca5644en.pdf`).

---

### Verbatim Group 2 Definition Clause
> **Table 1, Group 2: Processed culinary ingredients — Definition (p. 11)**  
> *"Substances obtained directly from group 1 foods or from nature by industrial processes such as pressing, centrifuging, refining, extracting or mining.*  
> *Used to prepare, season and cook group 1 foods.*  
> *May contain additives that prolong product duration, protect original properties or prevent proliferation of microorganisms."*

---

### Verbatim Group 2 Examples Clause
> **Table 1, Group 2: Processed culinary ingredients — Examples (p. 11)**  
> *"Vegetable oils crushed from seeds, nuts or fruit (notably olives); butter and lard obtained from milk and pork; sugar and molasses obtained from cane or beet; honey extracted from combs and syrup from maple trees; starches extracted from corn and other plants; vegetable oils with added anti-oxidants; salt mined or from seawater, and table salt with added drying agents.*  
> *Also includes products consisting of group 2 items, such as salted butter, and group 2 items with added vitamins or minerals, such as iodised salt."*

---

### Verbatim Section 2 Prose on Group 2
> **Section 2, Group 2: Processed culinary ingredients (p. 9)**  
> *"Processed culinary ingredients include oils, butter, lard, sugar and salt. See Table 1, below.*  
> *These are substances derived from group 1 foods or else from nature by processes such as pressing, refining, grinding, milling, and drying. Some methods used to make processed culinary ingredients are originally ancient. But now they usually are industrial products, designed to make durable products suitable for use in home, restaurant and canteen kitchens to prepare, season and cook freshly prepared dishes and meals."*

---

### Verbatim Group 4 Modification / Marker Clauses
> **Table 1, Group 4: Ultra-processed foods — Definition & Examples (p. 11–12)**  
> *"Ingredients include sugar, oils or fats, or salt, generally in combination, and substances that are sources of energy and nutrients that are of no or rare culinary use such as high fructose corn syrup, hydrogenated or interesterified oils, and protein isolates... Many ready-to-consume products such as... margarines and other spreads..."*

> **Section 2, Identifying Ultra-Processed Foods (p. 10)**  
> *"insoluble fibre, hydrogenated or interesterified oil. The presence in the list of ingredients of one or more of these food substances identifies a product as ultra-processed."*

## 2. Naming vs. Class-Instance Breakdown

| Category Clause / Item | Verbatim Scope in FAO 2019 | Inference Level | Status & Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Olive oil** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"notably olives"* | **Exact-named** | Olives explicitly cited in the vegetable oil clause |
| **Butter** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"butter and lard obtained from milk and pork"*; p. 9: *"oils, butter, lard"* | **Exact-named** | Butter is named verbatim in definition and examples |
| **Lard** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"butter and lard obtained from milk and pork"*; p. 9: *"oils, butter, lard"* | **Exact-named** | Lard is named verbatim in definition and examples |
| **Vegetable oils from seeds** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"crushed from seeds"* | **Class-instance** | Overarching class named; specific seed species (`sunflower oil`, `sesame oil`, `cottonseed oil`, `safflower oil`, `mustard oil`, `canola oil`, `soybean oil`, `flaxseed oil`, `corn oil`, `poppyseed oil`, `grapeseed oil`, `tomatoseed oil`, `teaseed oil`) are class-instances |
| **Vegetable oils from tree nuts** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"crushed from... nuts"* | **Class-instance** | Overarching class named; specific nut species (`peanut oil`, `walnut oil`, `almond oil`, `hazelnut oil`, `sheanut oil`, `babassu oil`) are class-instances |
| **Vegetable oils from fruit** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"crushed from... fruit"* | **Class-instance** | Overarching class named; specific oily fruits (`palm oil`, `coconut oil`, `avocado oil`) are class-instances |
| **Vegetable plant butters** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"crushed from seeds, nuts or fruit"*; p. 9: *"pressing, refining"* | **Class-instance** | Solid vegetable fats expressed from seeds/beans (`cocoa butter`, `nutmeg butter`, `ucuhuba butter`) are class-instances |
| **Animal fats other than lard** | Table 1 Group 2 p. 11: *"obtained from milk and pork"* | **Class-instance** | Clause names pork (lard); other animal fats (`beef tallow`, `mutton tallow`, `chicken fat`, `turkey fat`, `goose fat`) are class-instances of culinary animal fats |
| **Hydrogenated / interesterified oils** | Table 1 Group 4 p. 11; Section 2 p. 10: *"hydrogenated or interesterified oil"* | **Standing Rule** | Group 4 marker class 1; excluded from candidate staging |
| **Margarines & spreads** | Table 1 Group 4 p. 12: *"margarines and other spreads"* | **Exact-named** | Ultra-processed formulations; rejected |

## 3. Silence on Specific Terms (`SOURCE-SILENT`)

Where the FAO 2019 report text does not name or define a term or category, it is recorded strictly as **SOURCE-SILENT**:
- **Ghee**: **SOURCE-SILENT** in the FAO 2019 text (clarified butter is a traditional culinary fat, but unmentioned by name in the FAO text; handled in repository via Indian culinary literature citations).
- **Fish oils / Marine oils**: **SOURCE-SILENT** in Group 2 culinary ingredients. (Queued in Batch 7 pending policy decision).
- **Vanaspati**: **SOURCE-SILENT** in the FAO text (partially hydrogenated vegetable oil cooking medium; handled by Group 4 marker rules).
- **Cream as a fat**: **SOURCE-SILENT** as a Group 2 culinary fat in FAO text (mentioned as a Group 1 milk derivative / culinary cream; queued).
- **Fat fractions and grades**: **SOURCE-SILENT** in Group 2 (phospholipids/lecithin, diacylglycerols, stearin/olein fractions; queued).
- **Herbal / seed infusions & essential oils**: **SOURCE-SILENT** (fragrance/flavoring extracts; excluded).
