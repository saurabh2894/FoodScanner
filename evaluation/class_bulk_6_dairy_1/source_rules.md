# Category Source Rules: FDC 'Dairy and Egg Products' (Batch 6)

## 1. Primary Source Authority & Verbatim Quotations

All classification grounding is established from the primary report:
**FAO 2019**: Carlos Augusto Monteiro, Geoffrey Cannon, Mark Lawrence, Maria Laura da Costa Louzada, Priscila Pereira Machado. *Ultra-processed foods, diet quality, and health using the NOVA classification system*. Rome: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, 2019. (Report text `ca5644en.pdf`).

### Verbatim Group 1 Clauses
> **Table 1, Group 1: Unprocessed or minimally processed foods — Examples (p. 11)**  
> *"fresh, powdered or pasteurized milk;"*  
> *"fresh, powdered, chilled or frozen eggs;"*  
> *"fresh or pasteurized plain yoghurt;"*

### Verbatim Minimal Processing Methods Clause
> **Table 1, Group 1: Minimally processed foods — Definition (p. 11)**  
> *"Unprocessed foods altered by industrial processes such as removal of inedible or unwanted parts, drying, powdering, squeezing, crushing, grinding, fractioning, steaming, poaching, boiling, roasting, and pasteurization, chilling, freezing, placing in containers, vacuum packaging, non-alcoholic fermentation, and other methods that do not add salt, sugar, oils or fats or other food substances to the original food."*

### Group 2, Group 3, and Related Dairy Clauses
- **Butter**:
  > **Table 1, Group 2: Processed culinary ingredients — Examples (p. 11)**  
  > *"butter and lard obtained from milk and pork; ... Also includes products consisting of group 2 items, such as salted butter"*
- **Cheese**:
  > **Table 1, Group 3: Processed foods — Definition & Examples (p. 12)**  
  > Definition: *"and, in the case of breads and cheeses, using non-alcoholic fermentation."*  
  > Examples: *"freshly made unpackaged breads and cheeses."*  
  > Report text (p. 9): *"simple cheeses to which salt is added."*
- **Yogurt / Fermented Milk**:
  > **Table 1, Group 1: Examples (p. 11)**  
  > *"fresh or pasteurized plain yoghurt;"*  
  > Report text (p. 8 / p. 10): Fruit yogurt or yogurt with added sugar/flavors is ultra-processed (Group 4).
- **Whey and Casein**:
  > **Section 2: Ingredients characteristic of ultra-processed foods (p. 9)**  
  > *"sources of protein (hydrolysed proteins, soya protein isolate, gluten, casein, whey protein, and 'mechanically separated meat')."* (Group 4 marker class).
- **Ghee**:
  > **SOURCE-SILENT**. Clarified butterfat obtained from milk/butter; shares Group 2 basis with butter, but the specific word "ghee" is not named in FAO 2019.
- **Cream**:
  > **SOURCE-SILENT** in Group 1/2 table examples (only appears as "ice-cream" in ultra-processed products). Cream is a high-fat milk fraction (culinary ingredient basis).
- **Paneer**:
  > **SOURCE-SILENT**. Traditional South Asian fresh acid-coagulated curd/cheese; pending per-form decision.
- **Buttermilk**:
  > **SOURCE-SILENT**. Traditional butter-churning byproduct / cultured dairy beverage; pending per-form decision.

### Inference Levels
- **Cow milk** (`milk`, `whole milk`, `skim milk`, `milk powder`):  
  *Inference level: exact-named*. The clause explicitly names `"fresh, powdered or pasteurized milk"`.
- **Other animal milks** (`sheep milk`, `buffalo milk`, `human milk`):  
  *Inference level: class-instance of milk*. The clause names `"fresh, powdered or pasteurized milk"`; species variations are class-instances.
- **Chicken eggs** (`eggs`, `fresh eggs`, `frozen pasteurized eggs`):  
  *Inference level: exact-named*. The clause explicitly names `"fresh, powdered, chilled or frozen eggs"`.
- **Other avian eggs** (`duck eggs`, `goose eggs`, `quail eggs`, `turkey eggs`):  
  *Inference level: class-instance of eggs*.
