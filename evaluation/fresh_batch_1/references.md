# Item-level references (fresh batch 1) - written BEFORE any app run

Hand-prepared by an LLM reading each ingredient-panel photo. Not ground truth: spot-check against the photos (links in manifest.json). 'Top-level items' are the entries as printed, in order; a pair joined by 'and' or '&' that names two separate ingredients counts as two items. Expected NOVA group is provisional with its stated basis; 'unjudged' = no source I read settles it (excluded from classification scoring). N04 is excluded before scoring (variant of a development product).
