indian-alias-24: 12 aliases, counted separately from heads (0 new heads). Each pairs an Indian name with an English term already in the lexicon, using the standard's own section heading: FSSAI Food Products Standards Chapter 2.9 (https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_9_Salt%20Spices%20Condiments%20and%20related%20products.pdf): Turmeric (Haldi), Cardamom (Elaichi), Cinnamon (Dalchini), Cloves (Laung), Coriander (Dhania), Fennel (Saunf), Mace (Jaipatri), Nutmeg (Jaiphal), Saffron (Kesar), Caraway (Siahjira), Cumin (Zeera), Chillies and Capsicum (Lal Mirchi). Naming only. The pairing is the FSSAI heading's, scoped to the spice standard; it is not encoded as a universal synonym of any other sense (e.g. "Kesar mango" stays unrecognised). Not encoded (leads): methi (seed or leaf), sarson and rai (seed, greens or oil), jeera spelling (source says Zeera), taj, kalonji, sonth, the source was fetched truncated at ~50 KB so later sections of chapter 2.9 (e.g. asafoetida/hing, amchur) were not read.
