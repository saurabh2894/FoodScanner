# Item-level references (hand-prepared for spot-checking)

Prepared by an LLM reading each ingredient-panel photo BEFORE the app was run. They are NOT ground truth: please spot-check them against the photos (links in manifest.json). 'Top-level items' are the entries as printed, in order; brackets are kept as printed. Expected NOVA is a provisional judgement with its stated basis; 'unjudged' means no source settles it.
